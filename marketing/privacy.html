Privacy

Last updated: 2026-05-07

How koven.chat handles your data. If you sign up on a different Koven server, that operator runs their own policy.

What we store

  • Account. An email address (used only to send you the 6-digit login code), a username, a profile. No password.
  • Messages. Public-room messages live on the server in plaintext. DMs are end-to-end encrypted; we can't read them.
  • Governance events. Flags, votes, collapses, censures. All public, all forever, by design.
  • Reputation. Per-account reputation scores, derived from your activity. Computed locally. Not shared with third parties.

What we don't collect

  • No analytics. No tracking pixels. No third-party SDKs.
  • No telemetry. The client and the server don't phone home.
  • No payment data. koven.chat is free to use.

Federation

Koven federates with other Koven servers. Post to a federated room and your message is replicated to every server hosting a member of that room. Governance events go to the public log. If you don't want a message leaving koven.chat, post it in a non-federated or encrypted room.

Cookies

First-party storage to keep you signed in. No advertising or cross-site tracking cookies.

Deletion

Delete your account from the client. Your profile is removed and your username is detached from your messages. Governance events you signed in the past remain in the log under a redacted identifier — the log is append-only by design and there's no way to retroactively un-flag or un-vote. That's the cost of a tamper-evident record.
