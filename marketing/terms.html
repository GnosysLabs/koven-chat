Terms of Use

Last updated: 2026-05-07

Terms for the koven.chat instance. Other Koven servers run under their own terms.

What you agree to

Anything you post in a public room is visible to anyone in that room, including people on other federated Koven servers. Governance events you sign (flags, votes) are visible forever. That's the design.

Acceptable use

Use Koven for any speech lawful in your jurisdiction and ours. The community decides what stays visible through consensus. The platform doesn't moderate.

One exception: content that hits the floor_violation bar (CSAM, credible threats, doxxing) collapses without a vote. Non-negotiable. Repeated floor violations get the account suspended and reported to law enforcement where required.

What you keep, what we keep

  • You own what you write. By posting on a federated server you grant Koven a non-exclusive license to display, transmit, and federate it for the purpose of running the chat.
  • The source is MIT. Anything we add to the engine, gate, or client ships under the same license.
  • Governance events you sign are part of the public log. They can't be unsigned. That's the price of an honest record.

Account suspension

We suspend accounts for three reasons only: confirmed floor-violation activity, automation overwhelming the server, or compromise. Disagreement with what you said isn't on that list and never will be.

Self-hosting

Run your own Koven server, you're responsible for what's on your hardware. Set your own server-level rules within the documented protocol floors. Don't gate your server out of the federation while pretending to participate.

Disclaimers

Koven is provided "as is", no warranty. We're a small open-source project. We'll do our best to keep it running and fix bugs honestly. We don't promise uptime.

Changes

If these terms change in a substantive way, we'll post a notice on the home page and link the diff. The previous version stays in git forever.
